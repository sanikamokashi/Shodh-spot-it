१The story behind Shodh

A keepsake with a story

‘Shodh’ (meaning ‘Search’) is our very own home-made variation of the popular game ‘Spot It!’ with an Indian twist.

Spot-It is one of Parth and Rishi’s favorite games, and is a top contender for any family game night. We created this special custom deck as a heartwarming keepsake for Parth’s Munja ceremony. It contains symbols that represent the rich mythology, national elements, and daily life of India. We hope kids have a great time discovering and matching these beautiful illustrations!

२How to play

Spot it, say it, win it

A fast-paced match-finding game. It’s a wonderful way for children to sharpen their observation skills while learning the traditional names of each cultural symbol.

  1. १

    Turn over two cards

    Place one card face up in the middle. Every player keeps an eye on it and on the card in front of them.

  2. २

    Find the one match

    Any two cards share exactly one symbol. Sizes and angles change, so look carefully.

  3. ३

    Name it first

    Call out the symbol’s traditional name. The fastest person to spot the match wins that card.

३The math

Why there’s always exactly one match

57
symbols in the deck
8
symbols on every card
55

1
match between any two

The deck is built on a pattern mathematicians call a finite projective plane. With 8 symbols per card, the pattern allows 7 × 7 + 7 + 1 = 57 symbols and up to 57 cards, arranged so that every pair of cards shares one symbol, never zero and never two. Like the original game, Shodh uses 55 of them.
